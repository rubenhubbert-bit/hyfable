# HyFable Patreon — 6-Month Content Calendar

> Maps all 50 Meshy.ai prompts into themed monthly drops. Each month has a
> cohesive theme, a narrative hook, and a clear model lineup. With 4 retries per
> prompt, you'll select the best generation from each and have alternates for
> future bonus content.

## Alternate Retry Strategy

Each prompt gets 4 retries. Here's how to use all of them:

| Retry | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Best (#1)** | Primary release model — the hero of the monthly drop |
| **2nd Best** | Held for "Variant" releases in lighter months (5 and 6) |
| **3rd Best** | Potential Merchant-exclusive or loyalty reward content |
| **4th / Rejected** | Behind-the-scenes content — "rejected iterations" posts showing the generation process (great social content) |

This means your 50 prompts don't just produce 50 models — they produce **50 primary models + 50 variants + 50 exclusives + 50 BTS content pieces = 200 pieces of content.**
