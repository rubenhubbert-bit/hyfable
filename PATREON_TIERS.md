# HyFable Patreon — Tier Structure & Descriptions

> Pricing modeled after top-performing 3D model Patreons (One Page Rules, Hex3D,
> Titan Forge, Cinderwing3D, Nomnom Figures). The $10 price point is the proven
> sweet spot in this space. Start with 3 tiers — add more only after you learn
> what your audience responds to.

---

## Tier 1: Adventurer — $5/month

**Tagline:** *Step into the world of HyFable.*

**What you get:**
- 3-4 curated 3D models from each monthly themed drop (our favorites from the full set)
- Models delivered in OBJ and GLB formats
- Access to the HyFable Discord community with exclusive Adventurer channels
- Behind-the-scenes posts showing the creation process — prompts, iterations, and final picks
- Monthly poll vote on one model in the next release

**Who this is for:** Collectors, hobbyists, and fans who want a taste of each monthly release without the full library.

**Positioning notes:** This is your volume tier. The $5 price point has the lowest friction and the widest appeal. It exists to build your subscriber count and community. The behind-the-scenes content doubles as marketing — patrons share the process posts, which attracts new subscribers.

---

## Tier 2: Champion — $10/month

**Tagline:** *Unlock the full armory.*

**What you get:**
- **Everything in Adventurer, plus:**
- The FULL monthly themed drop (8-12 models per month)
- All models in multiple formats: OBJ, FBX, GLB, and STL (print-ready)
- Pre-supported STL files for resin printing (where applicable)
- Welcome Pack: instant access to 5 bonus models from past drops when you first subscribe
- Access to the back-catalog archive — every model ever released, organized by theme
- Early access: models delivered 48 hours before public posting
- Priority poll influence: your votes count double on monthly community polls

**Who this is for:** The core audience. Tabletop gamers, 3D printing enthusiasts, indie game devs, and digital artists who want the complete collection.

**Positioning notes:** This is your revenue engine. Based on top creators' data, most subscribers land here. The back-catalog archive is the key retention mechanic — the longer someone stays, the more value they've accumulated, making cancellation feel like a loss. The welcome pack gives instant gratification on day one.

---

## Tier 3: Merchant Lord — $25/month

**Tagline:** *Build your empire with HyFable.*

**What you get:**
- **Everything in Champion, plus:**
- **Commercial license** to sell physical prints made from HyFable models (license does NOT permit resale of the digital files themselves)
- Promotional image pack for each model (pre-rendered turntables, beauty shots, and social-ready thumbnails you can use in your store listings)
- Merchant-exclusive Discord channel for networking with other sellers, sharing tips, and coordinating
- Your store featured in our monthly "Merchant Spotlight" post to all patrons
- Input on upcoming model themes — direct message access for requests and suggestions
- Quarterly exclusive Merchant-only model not available at any other tier

**Who this is for:** Etsy sellers, convention vendors, small print-on-demand businesses, and anyone who wants to sell physical prints of HyFable models commercially.

**Positioning notes:** This is your high-margin tier. At $25/month, you're in line with the market (comparable Merchant tiers range $20-$50). The commercial license is the draw, but the promo materials and Merchant Spotlight create genuine additional value that justifies the jump from $10. The quarterly exclusive creates urgency and retention.

---

## Future Tier (Add After 6 Months)

### Tier 4: Patron Saint — $50/month

**Tagline:** *Shape the world of HyFable.*

**What you get:**
- **Everything in Merchant Lord, plus:**
- 1 custom model request per month (you describe it, we generate and curate it for you)
- Name/username credited in the monthly release notes as a Patron Saint
- Access to raw prompt libraries and generation notes for every model
- Exclusive quarterly "Director's Cut" bundle — experimental models, alternate textures, and style variants that didn't make the main release
- Private group call quarterly with the HyFable team

**When to launch:** Only add this tier once you have 100+ subscribers at the Champion level. It creates aspirational value — most people won't pay $50, but knowing it exists makes the $25 tier feel like a deal.

## Pricing Rationale

| Tier | Price | Industry Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adventurer | $5 | $3-$5 entry tiers (Brite Minis $5, Fantastic Plants $3.90) | Low friction, high conversion |
| Champion | $10 | $9-$15 full access (Hex3D $10, STL Miniatures $9, Aether Studios $15) | Market sweet spot |
| Merchant Lord | $25 | $20-$50 commercial (Forest Dragon $25, Nomnom $30) | Commercial license premium |
| Patron Saint | $50 | $50 top tier (mz4250 $50) | Whales and superfans |
